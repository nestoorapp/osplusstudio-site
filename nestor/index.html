A day with Nestor

Read it, understand it, do one thing with it.

Every day has the same shape, and nothing has to be typed.

  1. First

    Read the real thing

    A passage of Western philosophy or literature, word for word, with old or difficult words explained when you tap them.

  2. Then

    Meet the person

    The story of who wrote it, when and why. Then a few questions on what the passage actually says.

  3. Finally

    Do one thing today

    A short set of cards and a quiz, one concrete task for today, and a few questions to think about tonight.

The voyage

From Calypso's island to Ithaca.

Your ninety days are an Odyssey. Nestor, the wise counsellor, advises. You steer.

  1. Day 1

    Calypso's island

    Where the comfortable life keeps you. The voyage starts by leaving.

  2. Along the way

    Stops and trials

    Each leg ends at a new shore, with a trial and a seal to earn.

  3. Every week

    Nestor's review

    The questions you missed come back, so what you read stays with you.

  4. Day 90

    Ithaca

    Home, and a different person from the one who set out.

Your companion

Argos waits for you.

Odysseus's loyal dog waited twenty years for him to come home. In Nestor he keeps you company every day, cheers when you finish and naps when you're done.

He never scolds. Every seventh lesson earns a harbor day, so when life gets in the way, your streak holds.

Made with care

Honest about what it is.

  • Real texts

    Every passage is quoted word for word from a public-domain translation, with its source shown in the lesson.

  • Real paintings

    Paintings run through every lesson. Each one is in the public domain, with its artist, year and collection credited.

  • Your data stays yours

    No accounts and no servers. Your progress, notes and settings stay on your phone.
